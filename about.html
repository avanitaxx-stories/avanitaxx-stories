About Avanitaxx Stories

An independent online reading and writing community for original stories, authors, and readers.

Our purpose

Avanitaxx Stories is an independent online reading and writing website at avanitaxxstories.top. It provides a place to discover original stories, follow chapter updates, explore author profiles, and use reader and community features where available.

The website is presented under the AVANITAXX STORIES name. It features creative work published by AVANITAXX and may also feature invited or community authors whose work is accepted for publication or display.

What you can explore

  • Original stories, chapters, series, and reading recommendations.
  • Author profiles and public creator updates.
  • Reader accounts, personal libraries, reading progress, likes, comments, and replies where available.
  • Community features such as the Author Lounge, Community Chat, and Soundroom when enabled.

For readers and writers

Readers can browse available works and use account-based features where sign-in is required. Writers are responsible for ensuring they own or have permission to submit stories, images, artwork, audio, and other material. The website may review, moderate, feature, restrict, or remove material to protect readers, creators, and the service.

Independent website

Avanitaxx Stories is independent and is not affiliated with Google, GitHub, Supabase, or other third-party providers except where a service relationship is expressly described. Third-party names and services remain the property of their respective owners.

Contact and reports: The website currently displays Contact / Report guidance in its footer panel, but that panel is informational and does not submit a report. A dedicated support email or working report form must be added before it can be described as an active contact channel. Never send passwords or verification codes in a report.
