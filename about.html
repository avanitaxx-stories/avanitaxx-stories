About Avanitaxx Stories

An independent online reading and writing community for original stories, authors, and readers.

Our purpose

Avanitaxx Stories is an independent online reading and writing space created to help readers discover original stories, follow chapter updates, explore author profiles, and connect with a respectful community of readers and writers.

The website is built around the creative work published under the AVANITAXX name and may also feature invited or community authors whose work is accepted for publication or display. Our goal is to give stories and the people who create them a welcoming place to be discovered.

What you can explore

  • Original stories, chapters, series, and reading recommendations.
  • Author profiles and public creator updates.
  • Reader accounts, personal libraries, reading progress, likes, comments, and replies where available.
  • Community features such as the Author Lounge, Community Chat, and Soundroom when enabled.

For readers and writers

Readers can browse available works and use account-based features where sign-in is required. Writers are responsible for ensuring they own or have permission to submit the stories, images, artwork, and other material they provide. The website may review, moderate, feature, or remove material to protect the community and the rights of creators.

Independent project

Avanitaxx Stories is an independent website. Unless expressly stated otherwise, it is not affiliated with third-party platforms or service providers mentioned on the site.

Contact: For questions, account concerns, copyright reports, or community issues, use the Contact / Report feature available on the main website. Do not include passwords or other sensitive login credentials in a report.
