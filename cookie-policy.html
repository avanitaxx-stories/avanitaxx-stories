Cookie Policy

How Avanitaxx Stories uses essential browser storage and optional Google Analytics measurement.

1. What cookies and similar technologies are

Cookies are small files stored by a browser. Websites may also use local storage, session storage, pixels, tags, and similar technologies. Avanitaxx Stories uses browser storage for essential functions and may use optional analytics technologies when you consent.

2. Essential storage

Essential storage supports core website functions such as keeping a sign-in session active, remembering necessary interface state, and making requested features work. These functions may rely on browser storage and are not presented as optional analytics cookies. Disabling browser storage may cause parts of the website to stop working properly.

3. Analytics

Avanitaxx Stories includes Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-HLC422WJCL). The website's consent feature is designed to load the Google Analytics script only after you select “Accept analytics.” Analytics may collect information about website interactions and technical/browser details to help understand site usage. Google may process this information under its own terms and policies.

4. Your choices

When the consent notice appears, select “Accept analytics” to allow optional analytics, or “Decline optional” to keep analytics disabled through the site's consent feature. Your choice is saved in your browser. You can change it later using “Cookie Settings” in the website footer. Clearing browser storage may reset your choice and cause the notice to appear again.

5. Third-party services

Authentication, hosting, embedded resources, or future advertising features may involve third-party services that operate under their own privacy and cookie practices. This policy should be updated if additional analytics, advertising, or tracking tools are introduced.

6. Browser controls

You can also manage or delete cookies and site storage through your browser settings. Blocking all storage may affect sign-in, saved preferences, and other website features.

7. Contact and updates

For questions about this policy, use Contact / Report on the main website. We may update this page when the website's technology or services change.

The consent control on the current site covers the site's Google Analytics script. It does not automatically manage cookies set independently by every third-party resource or browser extension. Review third-party provider settings and documentation as needed.
