Cookie Policy

How Avanitaxx Stories uses essential browser storage, optional Google Analytics, and advertising technologies.

1. Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small files stored by a browser. Websites may also use local storage, session storage, pixels, tags, web beacons, and similar technologies. Avanitaxx Stories uses browser storage for essential site functions and may use analytics and advertising technologies through third-party services.

2. Essential browser storage

Essential storage supports core functions such as maintaining a sign-in session, remembering necessary interface state, and making requested features work. Blocking all browser storage may affect sign-in, reading progress, saved preferences, and other website features.

3. Google Analytics

The website includes Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-HLC422WJCL). The site's consent feature is designed to load the Analytics script only after you select “Accept analytics.” Selecting “Decline optional” keeps that script disabled through this consent feature. Your choice is saved in your browser and can be changed later using “Cookie Settings” in the website footer. Clearing browser storage may reset your choice.

4. Google AdSense and advertising cookies

The website includes a Google AdSense publisher tag. When ads are requested or served, Google and third-party vendors may place or read cookies and use web beacons, IP addresses, or similar identifiers to serve, measure, limit, and personalize ads. Google and its partners may use information from visits to this website or other websites to provide ads, subject to the user's settings and applicable law.

You can review or change Google's ad personalization choices at Google Ads Settings. You can also visit aboutads.info/choices for additional industry opt-out information.

Important distinction: the site's Accept analytics / Decline optional controls govern the Google Analytics script only. They do not automatically manage all AdSense cookies or cookies set independently by third-party resources. Where applicable laws or Google's requirements call for a certified consent management platform or additional consent signals, the site operator must implement the appropriate consent solution for affected visitors.

5. Third-party services

Authentication, hosting, embedded resources, analytics, and advertising may involve third-party services that operate under their own privacy and cookie practices. Their technologies may change. See the Privacy Policy for more information about data use and providers.

6. Browser controls

You can manage or delete cookies and site storage through your browser settings. Blocking all storage may affect sign-in and other features. Third-party opt-out tools may also be available, but their operation is controlled by the relevant provider.

7. Updates

This policy may be updated when the website's technology, providers, advertising configuration, or legal requirements change. The latest revision date appears below.
