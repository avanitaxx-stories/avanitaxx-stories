Privacy Policy

How Avanitaxx Stories handles account, profile, reading, community, technical, analytics, and advertising information.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains categories of information that may be processed when you visit or use Avanitaxx Stories. The information involved depends on the features you use and the services enabled on the website.

2. Information you provide

  • Account and sign-in information: account identifiers and email address handled through the authentication service, including Google sign-in if you choose it.
  • Profile information: pen name, display name, biography, avatar, banner, and social links you choose to provide.
  • Content and communications: stories, chapters, comments, replies, community messages, images, reports, and other material you submit.

3. Information created through use

Depending on features used, the website may process reading progress, story and chapter views, saved-library entries, likes, follows, notification records, timestamps, and other interaction data needed to operate reader and community features.

4. Technical information and storage

Browser storage and similar technologies support functions such as maintaining sign-in sessions, remembering settings, and operating site features. Hosting, authentication, database, storage, content delivery, security, analytics, and advertising providers may process technical information such as IP address, device and browser details, request logs, and diagnostic data as part of delivering their services.

5. Analytics

The website includes Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-HLC422WJCL). The site's preference control is designed to load the Google Analytics script only after a visitor selects “Accept analytics.” Selecting “Decline optional” prevents that script from being intentionally loaded by this consent feature. The preference is stored in the visitor's browser. This control is specific to the site's Google Analytics script; it is not a universal control for all third-party technologies.

6. Advertising and Google AdSense

The website includes a Google AdSense publisher tag associated with publisher ID pub-6402539951879372. When Google ads are requested or served, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, or similar identifiers to collect or receive information from this website and other sites. These technologies may be used to provide, measure, limit, and personalize advertising, subject to user settings, Google's policies, and applicable law. Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.

Users can review or change Google's advertising personalization settings at Google Ads Settings. Users may also learn about industry opt-out choices at aboutads.info. These controls are provided by third parties and their availability may vary by region, browser, and device.

The site's “Accept analytics” and “Decline optional” buttons control the Google Analytics script described above. They do not, by themselves, control all advertising cookies or establish consent for every jurisdiction. Where applicable law or Google's publisher requirements require a certified consent management platform or additional consent signals, the site operator must configure the appropriate solution before serving ads to affected users.

7. Why information is used

  • To create and maintain accounts and provide requested features.
  • To save reading progress, libraries, preferences, and notifications.
  • To display published stories, profiles, comments, and community activity.
  • To protect the website, moderate content, investigate reports, and prevent abuse.
  • To understand usage where analytics consent is provided and to support advertising where enabled.

8. Public information and service providers

Published stories, public author profiles, and posts in public areas may be visible to other visitors. Information may be processed by providers supporting the website, including GitHub Pages for hosting, Supabase for authentication/database/storage functions, Google for sign-in, analytics, or advertising when used, and content-delivery libraries used by the site. These providers may process information under their own terms and privacy documentation.

9. Retention and security

Information is retained for as long as reasonably needed to provide features, maintain records, address security or abuse issues, resolve disputes, or meet applicable legal requirements. No website can guarantee absolute security. Use a strong, unique password and do not share credentials.

10. Your choices and requests

You may choose what optional profile information to provide, stop using the site, sign out, and change the site's optional analytics preference through Cookie Settings in the website footer. You may also use Google's advertising settings and the industry opt-out resource linked above. To request access, correction, or deletion of account-related information, a working support/contact channel must be provided by the site operator; the current in-site Contact / Report panel is informational and does not submit requests.

11. Children

The website is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. A parent or guardian who believes a child has submitted personal information should use a verified contact channel published by the site operator.

12. Changes

This policy may be updated when website features, providers, advertising settings, or legal obligations change. The “Last updated” date indicates the latest revision.

This policy is a description of the site's intended practices, not legal advice or a guarantee about independent third-party processing. The site operator should confirm actual data flows, review provider terms, and obtain advice on laws applicable to the operator and audience before commercial launch.
